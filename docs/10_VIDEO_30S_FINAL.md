# CrewReady: 30-Second Video (Final Script)

**Hard limit:** 30.0 s. **Voiceover:** about 75 words. The visuals carry most of the story, so the voice only says what the screen can't.
**Follows:** the NASA Space Apps Bangladesh "30 Seconds of Glory" guide and the global judging rules (section 2).
**Rules:** English subtitles burned in · hosted publicly, no login · not uploaded to the NASA site.

## 6. Facts behind the claims (checked in the code)

- **Four bars:** Power, Air & Water, Food and Shield. Twinkle's tour says to keep all four above zero. The "You learn" lines come from each level's `learned` text, and the data tags from each level's `source` line, in `src/content/levels.ts`.
- **Level 2 storm:** `src/services/nasa.ts` reads NASA DONKI storms from the last 30 days. `src/engine/storm.ts` turns the strongest one into a Small, Medium or Large storm (10, 20 or 30 damage).
  - On 14 Nov 2026 there was one storm, on 31 Oct, at **Kp 6**. That gives a Medium storm and the **NASA LIVE** badge.
  - Record before **30 Nov**, or check the badge on the day. If no storm falls inside the 30 days, it says NASA ARCHIVE.
- **Level 6:** uses whichever storm is stronger, today's live one or the May 2024 archive storm.
- **Fly:** `src/fly/engine.ts` sets the speed from today's NOAA Kp: Kp 0–3 is slow, 4–6 is medium, 7+ is fast.
- **Mini-games:** the Space Arcade has five games: Star Match, Asteroid Blaster, Signal Focus, Gem Swap and Cosmic Merge (`src/minigames/arcade/engines.ts`). Stardust from the Arcade and Fly pays for hints (`src/screens/Play.tsx`).
- **Twinkle:** the help card and result headings use Gen Alpha lines (`src/components/Twinkle.tsx`), in English and Bangla.
